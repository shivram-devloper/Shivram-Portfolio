About

I build the web presence small businesses actually need.

Most of my work is for owner-run businesses — cafés, salons, repair shops, and local services — who need a website that looks professional, loads quickly, and is simple for their customers to use on a phone.

I focus on the fundamentals: clear structure, readable typography, honest content, and layouts that hold up on any screen size. Every project starts with understanding the business before a single line of code gets written.

My approach is deliberately straightforward — semantic HTML, clean CSS, and JavaScript that's used only where it genuinely improves the experience. No unnecessary frameworks, no bloated page weight.
